## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor (mandatory; scored by the methodology seat only). From an editorial-oversight view, this dimension asks whether the research design, the construction and cleaning of the data, the statistical reporting, and the materials a reader would need to reproduce the work clear the bar a peer reviewer in this field would apply. I do not score it; I would only note methodological issues where they spill into over-claiming or venue fit.

D2 domain_accuracy (mandatory; scored by the domain seat only). This dimension checks that the manuscript's claims are consistent with the current body of evidence, that prior studies are described faithfully, and that field-specific terms and reported results contain no factual errors. Not mine to score.

D3 argumentative_coherence (mandatory; scored by the devil's advocate and methodology seats). This dimension asks whether the central thesis hangs together internally, whether the evidence actually carries the claims made from it, and whether any reasoning fallacy undermines the main argument. Not mine to score, though a title/abstract/conclusion mismatch is also a structural and venue-fit matter that I will assess under D5/D6.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority; scored by the perspective seat only). This dimension asks whether framing, definitions, and implications are readable by people in neighbouring fields and whether any claim reaching beyond the home discipline is supported. Not mine to score.

D5 writing_and_structure (normal priority; mine). This dimension asks whether the manuscript is well organised, clearly written, has exhibits of adequate quality that stand on their own, and follows the venue's conventions (for an Insights article: word ceiling net of exhibits, exhibit cap, one-page exhibits, abstract length, keywords, title page elements, self-containment without the supplemental appendix, required statements).

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory; mine). This dimension asks whether the paper belongs in the configured outlet and whether it offers an original and significant contribution suited to that outlet's readers. For a short-format finance outlet this means one clear, well-identified, interesting finding that a general finance readership would value, positioned correctly against existing work and not over-claimed.

Failure conditions. F1: if any mandatory dimension receives a fatal block from any seat, the panel decision is reject. F2: any block on a mandatory dimension from any seat forces at least major revision. F3: if a majority of seats score two or more mandatory dimensions warn or worse, major revision. F4: any block on a high-priority dimension forces major revision. F5: any warn or worse on any dimension forces at least minor revision. F0: accept only when every dimension is scored pass by all seats. Only the synthesizer applies these conditions; my role is to supply categorical judgements tied to pre-committed triggers.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Logical ordering (question, data, design, result, interpretation) with each section doing one job; prose that states the finding plainly; exhibits that are self-explanatory with complete notes, units, sample sizes and legible axes, each fitting on one page; internal consistency of numbers across text, abstract and exhibits; compliance with Insights conventions (word limit net of 200 words per exhibit, at most 5 exhibits, abstract of 100 words or fewer, up to 7 keywords, title page items, running title under 40 characters, data availability and AI disclosure placement, data citations in the references); main text self-contained without the Internet Appendix.
what_triggers_block: A hard venue rule is violated in a way that would cause desk return (word count above the net limit, more than five exhibits, an exhibit longer than one page) or essential design/variable information exists only in the Internet Appendix so that the main paper is not self-contained, or numbers conflict across text and exhibits on a headline estimate.
what_triggers_warn: Fixable presentation defects that do not trigger desk return: abstract over 100 words, missing keywords or title-page statements, exhibits lacking complete notes or readable labels, minor numeric inconsistencies on non-headline quantities, jargon or undefined abbreviations, or disorganised sections that obscure the single message.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: A single sharply stated finding of interest to a general finance readership; a clear statement of what is new relative to the closest existing studies on ESG rating initiation, coverage selection and valuation; whether the emerging-market setting adds insight rather than only geography; whether the headline claim (title, abstract, conclusion) is supported at the strength stated; whether the short format suits the contribution rather than being a compressed long paper.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is materially overstated relative to what the evidence supports (title or abstract asserts a causal or timing claim the design cannot deliver), or novelty relative to closely related published work is not established, requiring substantial reframing or new analysis before the paper could fit the outlet.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is real and fits the outlet but its positioning is incomplete, the significance for general finance readers is under-argued, or selected phrasing over-reaches in ways correctable by rewriting without new analysis.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper has no identifiable original contribution for this outlet (the finding is already established in published work or is outside the journal's scope), or the core claim is irreparably unsupported, so that no feasible revision could make it publishable at this venue.
