## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor (mandatory; scored by the methodology seat). The design, data construction, statistical reporting and reproducibility material must meet the ordinary peer-review standard of empirical finance. From my cross-disciplinary seat I do not score this dimension. Institutional facts about the markets studied (trading rules, foreign-ownership caps, index rebalancing calendars) can still bear on whether the design is credible, so I may raise such facts as prose for the methodology seat and the synthesizer to weigh.

D2 domain_accuracy (mandatory; scored by the domain seat). Claims must match current evidence in the domain, prior work must be represented correctly, and domain terms and results must be stated without factual error. I do not score it. Where a claim concerns the institutional environment of the exchanges studied, I report errors I can verify as findings and leave the scoring to the domain seat.

D3 argumentative_coherence (mandatory; scored by the devil's-advocate and methodology seats). The central thesis must hold together internally, and the evidence must support what is claimed without fallacies that sink the main argument. I do not score it and I do not check logic or internal consistency. My contribution is limited to alternative institutional explanations of the same pattern.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance (high priority; my dimension). Readers from adjacent fields must be able to follow the framing, definitions and implications: emerging-markets finance, market microstructure, accounting and disclosure regulation, index and investor-access policy, and practitioners at exchanges, regulators, listed firms and investors. Any claim that reaches past the paper's own discipline, such as policy advice to exchanges or regulators, investor guidance, or generalisation from the markets studied to other emerging or developed markets, must be supported by evidence and by the institutional context of those markets.

D5 writing_and_structure (normal priority; scored by the journal-fit seat). Organisation, clarity, exhibit quality and compliance with venue conventions such as length and exhibit limits. I do not score it.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory; scored by the journal-fit seat). The paper must suit the configured venue and add an original contribution that matters to its readers. I do not score it, although my comments on practical relevance may inform that seat.

Failure conditions. The synthesizer, not me, applies these panel rules. F1: any fatal block on a mandatory dimension leads to rejection. F2: any block on a mandatory dimension leads to major revision. F3: a majority finding two or more mandatory dimensions at warn or worse leads to major revision. F4: a block on any high-priority dimension leads to major revision, and D4 is such a dimension, so a block from me alone is decision-relevant. F5: any warn or worse leads at least to minor revision. F0: acceptance requires every dimension to pass. My block/warn/pass on D4 must therefore rest on anchored evidence, because a block triggers F4 without any other seat.

## Scoring Plan

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance
dimension_id: D4
what_to_look_for: Whether the paper describes the institutional setting of each market it studies (exchange structure, foreign-ownership limits, index and ESG-index inclusion rules, local sustainability-disclosure mandates, identity and selection rules of the rating provider); whether its key constructs are defined so that readers outside sustainable finance can follow them; whether implications for investors, listed firms, exchanges and regulators follow from and stay within the evidence; whether heterogeneity across markets is reported or discussed; and whether generalisations beyond the sample are bounded and justified.
what_triggers_block: Headline practical or policy implications, or claims of generalisation beyond the sample, contradict or clearly exceed the paper's own evidence, or depend on an institutional premise about the studied markets that is verifiably false, so that an adjacent-field reader or practitioner acting on the stated implications would be misled.
what_triggers_warn: Institutional context, construct definitions or cross-market heterogeneity are thin or missing, or implications for specific stakeholders are asserted without discussion of feasibility or boundaries, but the core implications remain supportable once context and scope limits are added.
