## Contract Paraphrase

D1 methodology_rigor (mandatory; scored by the methodology seat only). From an adversarial angle, this asks whether the study design, sample construction, data handling, statistical reporting and replication affordances would survive a hostile referee in empirical finance: could the estimated effect be an artefact of how the sample, event window, comparison group or standard errors were chosen, and could an outsider reproduce the numbers from what is disclosed? I do not score it, but I may report design flaws that break the argument.

D2 domain_accuracy (mandatory; scored by the domain seat only). This asks whether the paper's claims fit the current body of evidence on ESG ratings, rating initiation and firm value, whether prior studies are represented fairly rather than selectively, and whether domain terms and facts (rating providers, coverage rules, market institutions) are used correctly. Adversarially: is the literature framed so that the paper's result looks more novel or more settled than it is? Not scored by me.

D3 argumentative_coherence (mandatory; scored by me as owner, co-eligible with methodology). This asks whether the central thesis holds together: whether each inferential step from evidence to conclusion is valid, whether the headline claim (including any timing or causal language in the title and abstract) is what the evidence actually shows, whether the text contradicts itself or its own tables, and whether rival explanations that fit the same pattern are ignored. A fallacy or leap that undermines the core claim is the failure mode.

D4 cross_disciplinary_relevance (high; perspective seat only). This asks whether definitions, framing and implications can be followed by readers from adjacent fields (accounting, sustainability, policy, regional economics) and whether any cross-field claims are substantiated rather than asserted. Adversarially: do the stated implications for regulators, investors or rating agencies outrun the evidence? Not scored by me.

D5 writing_and_structure (normal; EIC seat only). This asks whether the manuscript is organised and written clearly, whether figures and tables are legible and self-explanatory, and whether the manuscript follows the venue's conventions (for this venue, length and exhibit limits, abstract length, required statements). Not scored by me.

D6 venue_fit_and_contribution (mandatory; EIC seat only). This asks whether the paper belongs in the configured venue and adds an original, significant contribution for its readers, i.e. passes the "so what" test relative to existing work. Adversarially: would the finding change what anyone believes or does? Not scored by me, though "so what" challenges remain within my findings channel.

Failure conditions, paraphrased: F1 (severity 95) any mandatory dimension receiving a fatal block from any seat leads to reject. F2 (90) any mandatory dimension scored block by any seat leads to major revision. F3 (70) a majority finding that two or more mandatory dimensions are warn or worse leads to major revision. F4 (60) any high-priority dimension (D4) scored block leads to major revision. F5 (40) any dimension warn or worse leads to minor revision. F0 (10) every dimension pass from all seats leads to accept. The highest-severity condition that fires governs; only the synthesizer applies them.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether the headline thesis (including timing and any causal wording in title, abstract and conclusion) follows step by step from the reported estimates; internal contradictions between text, tables and figures; hidden assumptions in the logic chain; selective emphasis of supportive specifications; overgeneralization beyond the sample; and whether plausible rival explanations for the same pattern are acknowledged and argued against.
what_triggers_block: A core claim in the title, abstract or conclusion is stated more strongly than the evidence supports (for example causal or mechanism language resting only on correlational or pre-trend patterns, or a timing claim that key specifications do not uniformly support), or a salient rival explanation that fits the same pattern is not addressed, such that the central argument needs substantial re-framing or new analysis to stand, while a defensible core remains.
what_triggers_warn: The core logic chain holds but has repairable gaps: isolated overstatement in secondary claims, unacknowledged hidden assumptions, rival explanations mentioned without adequate argument, minor text-table inconsistencies, or generalization that modestly outruns the sample, none of which overturn the headline conclusion.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper's own reported evidence contradicts its central conclusion, or the central conclusion rests on a logically invalid inference that no re-framing within the existing evidence can rescue, so that the core thesis cannot survive revision.
