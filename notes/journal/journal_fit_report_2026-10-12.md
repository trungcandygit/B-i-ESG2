## Journal notes (evidence)
- Global Finance Journal: scope "particularly interested in original articles that deal with environmental, social and
  governance (ESG) factors in financial decision making and their impact on valuation", sustainable and climate finance
  (source-backed: shop.elsevier.com/journals/global-finance-journal/1044-0283). Precedent: Sahin et al. (2023), GFJ 56,
  100780 (source-backed, notes/05). Positioning: lead with valuation consequences of entering ESG coverage. Verdict: first target.
- Journal of Sustainable Finance & Investment: aims cover theoretical and empirical sustainable finance, ESG integration
  into corporate policies and portfolios, ESG disclosure; double-anonymized review; optional gold OA (source-backed:
  tandfonline.com/journals/tsfi20/about-this-journal). SJR value from scijournal.org (unverified). Verdict: first or
  second target; strongest fit with the reframed v4 and a different publisher from SFR's audience.
- Pacific-Basin Finance Journal: aims "specialized forum for ... research on capital markets within the Asia-Pacific
  region", excludes US-only data (source-backed: shop.elsevier.com/journals/pacific-basin-finance-journal/0927-538X).
  Verdict: second wave; emphasize the five-market Asia-Pacific evidence and the econometric care.
- Emerging Markets Review: 2015 Elsevier archive page (premier vehicle for emerging-markets finance; CSR among topics)
  (inferred for current scope). Verdict: second wave after checking the current guide.
- Journal of Business Ethics: precedent source-backed through two verified references in the paper. Verdict: stretch;
  would need a stakeholder/ethics framing of rater selection.
- Asia-Pacific Journal of Financial Studies: no submission fee (unverified, third-party notice). Verdict: backup.
