# Journal of Emerging Market Finance (Sage): submission guidelines, partial (2026-10-12)
Sage domains (journals.sagepub.com, sagepub.com) are not reachable from this environment; facts below come from
search-result snippets of the official pages and must be checked at
https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/emf before submission.
- Scope: theory and practice of finance in emerging markets (source-backed, Sage page).
- Abstract: not more than 100 words; up to 6 keywords after the title page (older Sage page, snippet).
- Double spacing throughout, including tables, notes, references (older page, snippet).
- Peer review: double-anonymized; subscription journal; preprints not accepted (Sage guidelines page, snippet).
  Note: our manuscript is not posted as a preprint; keep it that way.
- Submission: Sage online peer-review portal (newer listing); older page gives jemf@ifmr.ac.in.
- Non-conforming manuscripts may be returned.
- Not found: word limit, reference style, submission fee, figure limit.
Implications for Stage 5: unstructured abstract of at most 100 words (current structured abstract is 250 words);
6 keywords (we have 6); anonymized main file plus separate title page; double spacing.
