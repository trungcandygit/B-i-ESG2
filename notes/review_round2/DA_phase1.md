## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): from an adversarial standpoint, this dimension asks whether the design, the data handling, the statistical reporting, and the reproducibility affordances would survive a hostile referee in the field. I do not score it, but any design weakness that lets a rival explanation produce the same estimates is a weakness I will report as a finding against the argument.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat): this dimension asks whether the claims agree with what the domain already knows, whether prior studies are represented faithfully, and whether domain terms and facts are used correctly. I do not score it, but a misrepresented literature or a mislabeled construct that props up the thesis is fair game for a counter-argument.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the DA seat): this is the dimension I score. It asks whether the central thesis holds together internally, whether each inferential step from evidence to conclusion is licensed, and whether any fallacy, unacknowledged rival explanation, or data-conclusion mismatch undermines the main claim. My job is to build the strongest case that the headline conclusion does not follow from the reported evidence.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, owned by the perspective seat): this dimension asks whether readers from adjacent fields can follow the framing, definitions, and implications, and whether claims that reach across fields are actually substantiated. I do not score it, but implications that outrun the evidence or that are asserted for audiences the analysis never touches are part of the logic chain I attack.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by the editor seat): this dimension covers organisation, clarity, exhibit quality, and conformity to venue conventions. I do not score it; I note presentation problems only where they hide or distort the logic of the argument.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the editor seat): this dimension asks whether the work belongs in the configured venue and offers an original, significant contribution for its readers. I do not score it, but the "so what" test is part of my remit, so I will challenge whether the claimed contribution is the contribution the evidence actually delivers.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Trace the headline claim back through each inferential step to the specific estimates that carry it; check that title, abstract, introduction, results, and conclusion state the same claim with the same strength; test whether a simpler rival explanation (selection, mechanical timing, sample composition, reverse causality, measurement artifact) fits the reported estimates at least as well; check that null results are not read as evidence of absence and that significant results are not read as causal when the design does not license it.
what_triggers_block: The main conclusion as stated in title, abstract, or conclusion is not supported by the paper's own primary estimates, or a rival explanation the paper does not rule out fits the evidence at least as well and would reverse the interpretation, but the defect is repairable by reframing or by analyses the existing data permit.
what_triggers_warn: The core claim survives, but one or more inferential steps are overstated, internally inconsistent across sections, or leave a plausible rival explanation only partly addressed, so the framing needs tightening without changing the substantive conclusion.
what_triggers_fatal: The paper's own data directly contradict its central claim, or the central claim rests on a circular or definitional construction such that no revision within the existing design could make the conclusion follow from the evidence.
