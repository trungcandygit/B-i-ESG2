# Reviewer 3 (Perspective) — Phase 2 report

**Strengths.** Clear, accessible framing; the practical lesson for researchers (TWFE vs. robust estimator) is well
communicated; external validity is limited to the five markets.

**Weaknesses.**
- R3-W1 [D4, warn] Institutional drivers of the waves. The paper documents market-specific waves (Thailand and
  Singapore 2019–2020; Malaysia 2021–2022) but offers no institutional account. Without verified sources on why
  the vendor expanded in each market, the paper should at least show the waves descriptively (cohort by market) in
  an appendix and state that the reasons are not observed.
- R3-W2 [D4, minor] Implications for investors and exchanges could be one or two sentences more concrete: e.g.,
  that coverage is not a cheap way to raise valuation for listed firms, and that rated/unrated comparisons in these
  markets are confounded by growth.
