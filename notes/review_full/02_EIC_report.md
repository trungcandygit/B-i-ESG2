# Journal-Fit Reviewer (EIC) — Phase 2 report

**Summary.** The paper uses the staggered first appearance of LSEG ESG scores for 675 non-financial firms in five
Southeast Asian markets and a Callaway–Sant'Anna design to ask whether rating coverage changes valuation. It finds
no post-coverage change in log MTB (0.002, CI −0.046 to 0.048) and a pre-coverage run-up in market capitalization
and MTB; a static TWFE regression would instead suggest a 6.6 percent effect.

**Strengths.** (1) One question, one message, suitable for the Insights format. (2) Pre-analysis plan committed
before estimation; all robustness checks reported including a failing placebo. (3) Exhibits are clean and each fits
one page; notes are short. (4) The TWFE contrast (Table 3, row R6) gives the paper a methodological lesson that
readers outside ESG will find useful.

**Weaknesses.**
- EIC-W1 [D6, warn] Contribution framing: the claim "coverage follows firm value" is intuitive; the paper must show
  why the magnitude and the post-coverage null matter, and must address the obvious alternative that coverage
  simply tracks index membership or size thresholds (anchor: Section 1 ¶2, Section 4.1 ¶2). Fix: state the
  selection mechanism explicitly, connect to the index-inclusion literature, and quantify how much of the
  cross-sectional valuation gap in Table 1 is pre-existing versus accrued in the run-up.
- EIC-W2 [D6, warn] Evidence depth: at 2,790 words against a 6,200-word allowance the paper leaves room for the
  sensitivity analysis that referees will ask for (trend-robust bounds, conditioning on pre-coverage growth). Fix:
  add these in the main text or an Internet Appendix.
- EIC-W3 [D5, pass-with-minor] Equation (1) is written in words inside a display line ("mean over scored firms
  of"); use notation with the average written as (1/N~g~)Σ. The title page lists ORCIDs; fine for single-anonymized
  review. Keywords and JEL present.

**Recommendation signal:** Major revision (contribution needs sharper framing and sensitivity evidence).
