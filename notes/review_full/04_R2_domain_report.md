# Reviewer 2 (Domain) — Phase 2 report

**Strengths.** Correct representation of Tsang et al. (2024) and Bikmetova and Pirinsky (2026) as U.S. evidence on
conduct; appropriate use of Berg et al. (2022); asset-pricing motivation (Merton, 1987; Pástor et al., 2021;
Pedersen et al., 2021) is accurate.

**Weaknesses.**
- R2-W1 [D2, warn] Score history. Berg, Fabisik, and Sautner (ECGI Finance Working Paper 708/2020) document
  widespread retroactive rewriting of Refinitiv (now LSEG) ESG scores. If historical scores were added or revised
  after the fact, the "first year with a score" in a later data download may predate the first public score, and
  initial-score levels (used in the H3 split) may be rewritten values. This is a first-order construct-validity
  issue for "coverage initiation" and must be discussed; the direction of the resulting bias should be stated.
- R2-W2 [D2, warn] Missing literature on index inclusion. The selection story ("providers add firms that became
  large enough to enter indices") connects to classic evidence on index additions (Shleifer, 1986; Harris and
  Gurel, 1986). Cite and use it to frame the alternative that price effects around inclusion are concentrated at
  the inclusion event, which annual data cannot isolate.
- R2-W3 [D2, minor] Terminology: say once that LSEG ESG scores were formerly Refinitiv (ASSET4) scores, since most
  prior work uses the older name.
